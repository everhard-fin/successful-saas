# Connecting Slack

Go to **Integrations**, then select **Connect** on the Slack card. Slack asks you to pick a workspace and approve access.

## "Couldn't connect to Slack" (admin approval required)

Your Slack workspace only lets admins approve new apps. Until a Slack admin approves Successful SaaS, every connection attempt fails, so clicking **Try again** won't help.

1. Ask a Slack workspace admin to open **Slack → Tools & settings → Manage apps → Requests**.
2. They approve **Successful SaaS**.
3. Go back to **Integrations** and select **Connect** once more.

The connection usually works within a minute of approval. Nothing else needs changing on your Successful SaaS account.

## Slack was connected but stopped posting

Someone may have removed the app from Slack, or the channel was archived. Disconnect Slack under **Integrations**, then connect it again and pick an active channel.
