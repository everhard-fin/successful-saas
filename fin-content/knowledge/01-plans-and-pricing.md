# Successful SaaS plans and pricing

Every Successful SaaS plan includes a monthly allowance of API calls. Your allowance resets at the start of each billing cycle.

| Plan | Monthly API calls | Price | When you reach the limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 50,000 | $99/month | Calls are throttled |
| Growth | 200,000 | $299/month | Calls are throttled |
| Scale | 1,000,000 | $899/month | Extra calls are billed at $0.90 per 1,000 |
| Enterprise | 5,000,000+ | Custom pricing | Custom limits agreed with our sales team |

## What's included

**Starter.** Core API, email support, 2 API keys.

**Growth.** Core API, webhooks, priority email support, 10 API keys.

**Scale.** Everything in Growth, plus single sign-on (SSO) and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

**Enterprise.** A dedicated customer success manager (CSM), a 99.99% uptime SLA and audit logs. Contact our sales team for a quote.

## Which plan is right for me?

- **Under 50,000 calls a month:** Starter.
- **Up to 200,000 calls a month, or you need webhooks:** Growth.
- **Up to 1,000,000 calls a month, or you need SSO or an SLA:** Scale. Scale never throttles you. If you go over, you just pay for the extra calls.
- **More than 1,000,000 calls a month, or you need audit logs or a dedicated CSM:** Enterprise.

You can upgrade at any time from **Billing** at https://billing.example.com. Upgrades take effect immediately. See *Upgrading or changing your plan*.
