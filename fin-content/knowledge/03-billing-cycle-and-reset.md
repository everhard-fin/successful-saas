# Billing cycles and usage resets

Your API call allowance is monthly. It resets to zero at the start of each billing cycle.

## When does my usage reset?

Your reset date is on your **Overview** page under **Plan & usage**, for example "Resets on Oct 5 · in 4 days". Your billing cycle starts on the same day each month.

## What happens at reset

- Your call count goes back to 0.
- Any throttling or blocking stops straight away.
- On Scale, overage from the previous cycle is added to that cycle's invoice.

## Does upgrading change my reset date?

No. Upgrading changes your limit straight away, but your billing cycle and reset date stay the same.

## Can my usage be reset early?

No. To get more calls before your reset date, upgrade your plan at https://billing.example.com.
