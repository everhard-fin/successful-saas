# Enterprise plan

Enterprise is for teams that need more than 1,000,000 API calls a month, or that need enterprise controls.

## What's included

- 5,000,000+ API calls a month, with limits sized to your needs
- A dedicated customer success manager (CSM)
- 99.99% uptime SLA
- Audit logs
- Everything in Scale, including SSO

## Pricing

Enterprise pricing is custom and set by our sales team based on your volume and requirements. It can't be bought online.

## Moving from Scale to Enterprise

Scale customers who regularly go over 1,000,000 calls often save money on Enterprise, compared with paying $0.90 per 1,000 overage calls every month.

To get started, ask us to connect you with sales. We'll share your current plan and usage, so you don't have to explain it again.
