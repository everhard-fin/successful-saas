# Upgrading or changing your plan

## How to upgrade

1. Go to **Billing** at https://billing.example.com.
2. Choose **Change plan** and pick your new plan.
3. Confirm. That's it.

## When does the new plan start?

**Straight away.** Your new monthly limit applies to the current billing cycle the moment you confirm. If you were throttled or blocked, that stops within a minute.

Example: you're on Starter and have used 48,200 of your 50,000 calls. You upgrade to Growth. Your limit is now 200,000, so you have 151,800 calls left this cycle.

## What will I pay?

You pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current cycle. After that, you pay the new plan's full monthly price. Your reset date doesn't change.

## Recommended next plan

| Current plan | Next plan | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic (Legacy) | Starter | 25,000 → 50,000 calls, $49 → $99/month, throttled instead of blocked |
| Starter | Growth | 50,000 → 200,000 calls, $99 → $299/month, adds webhooks |
| Growth | Scale | 200,000 → 1,000,000 calls, $299 → $899/month, no throttling (overage billed) |
| Scale | Enterprise | 5,000,000+ calls, custom pricing, contact sales |

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
